# Knowledge Base and Reference Governance

## 1. Purpose

The knowledge module is a professional reference and preparation aid. It must distinguish general educational explanations, user-entered assumptions, lender-specific instructions and verified legal/statutory sources.

## 2. Knowledge article fields

Each article should support:
- Article code and title.
- Topic and subtopic.
- Jurisdiction (default India).
- Entity applicability.
- Industry applicability.
- Article type: concept, procedure, formula, checklist, statutory reference, lender practice, worked example or caution.
- Plain-language explanation.
- Formula and variable definitions where relevant.
- Worked example clearly labelled illustrative.
- Limitations / exceptions.
- Source title, issuing authority, URL or document reference.
- Publication/issue date and effective-from / effective-to dates where applicable.
- Last verified date, reviewer and verification status.
- Version and superseded-by reference.

## 3. Source hierarchy and claims

Use authoritative primary sources where the content concerns current law, tax, regulation, accounting standards, government schemes or lender requirements. Examples include legislation/rules/notifications, official government or regulator publications, official standard-setter material and written lender documentation.

Secondary publications may help explain concepts but must be labelled as secondary. Unverified web pages, forum answers and AI-generated summaries must not be presented as authoritative sources.

Each sourced claim should be linked to its source record. Record source date and effective date separately; a publication date does not by itself prove that a rule is currently effective.

## 4. Tax and legal content safeguards

- Do not hardcode tax rates, depreciation rates, thresholds, subsidy eligibility or statutory rules without a versioned source and effective period.
- Do not infer a person's tax treatment from entity label alone; tax profile and relevant facts are required.
- Tax calculators in the first release should clearly state whether they are indicative estimates or complete calculations.
- Distinguish accounting depreciation from income-tax depreciation.
- Distinguish GST-inclusive customer collection from net revenue and output tax.
- Treat ambiguous or mixed tax supplies as review-required unless a component-wise method is implemented and supported.
- Do not generate legal opinions, audit opinions, certificates or professional certifications.
- Show the last verification date and source citation for any current-law reference included in a generated report.

## 5. Ratio and lender benchmark policy

A ratio article must include formula, meaning, use, denominator conventions, data requirements and known limitations. Benchmarks must be stored as configurable lender/sector-specific guidance with source and effective date. Do not present one universal DSCR, current ratio or debt-equity threshold as mandatory for all banks and industries.

## 6. AI-assisted content policy

Any future AI-assisted project narrative feature must:
- Use only the project's approved inputs and explicitly identified research sources.
- Separate verified facts from assumptions and suggested wording.
- Provide source links and access dates for external factual claims.
- Never invent local market demand, competitor counts, approvals, costs, promoter experience or industry statistics.
- Require user review and acceptance before inclusion in a final report.
- Avoid transmitting confidential client data to an external AI service unless explicitly enabled and approved.

## 7. Knowledge review workflow

Draft → source attached → technical/professional review → verified → active → periodic re-verification → superseded/archived.

An expired or superseded statutory source must not silently remain the active source for a new financial year. Older calculation runs retain the historical source versions used at the time.
