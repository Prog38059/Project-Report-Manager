# Product Goals and Boundaries

## Product goal
Build a professional Windows desktop application for CA/accounting professionals to prepare entity-aware, industry-neutral bank/project reports with integrated projections, loan schedules, appraisal metrics and professional PDF/Word/Excel outputs.

## Initial legal constitution coverage
Individual, sole proprietorship, HUF, partnership firm, LLP and private limited company. These categories must not be treated as legally interchangeable. The model must support constitution-specific member, capital, appropriation and statement-presentation requirements.

## Initial industry/template coverage
Manufacturing, trading, hospital/clinic, hotel, restaurant, EV charging, transport/logistics, education, rental/commercial property and service businesses. Templates are input accelerators; they do not hardcode accounting policy.

## User-approved defaults
- Seven annual projection years.
- Historical financial information hidden/disabled by default and enabled only when required for a particular project.
- Horizontal annual statements: A = serial, B = note/schedule reference, C = particulars, D onward = projected years. Seven years occupy D:J.
- Annual financial statements by default; monthly loan repayment schedules included.
- Single-computer Windows application initially; future LAN version is a separate design/approval phase.
- Comprehensive scope delivered in controlled phases.

## Core outcomes
1. Client/entity/project management.
2. Dynamic project cost and means-of-finance schedules.
3. Industry-aware revenue and expense forecasting.
4. Fixed-asset and depreciation schedules.
5. Multiple loan facilities and repayment methods.
6. Working-capital modelling.
7. Integrated P&L, Balance Sheet and Cash Flow.
8. Ratios, lender-configurable DSCR and project viability analysis.
9. Scenario and sensitivity analysis.
10. Validation, reconciliations and immutable calculation snapshots.
11. Professional PDF, Word and Excel outputs from one calculation run.
12. Integrated knowledge base with source/effective-date governance.

## Non-goals for first release
- Multi-user LAN deployment.
- Automatic tax/legal conclusions without verified versioned source data.
- Automatic approval or sanction recommendation.
- Audit opinions, certificates or professional attestations.
- Unreviewed AI-generated market research or legal/tax content.
- Automatic filing/submission to banks or government portals.

## Success criteria
A user can create a project, enter supported assumptions, produce reproducible seven-year financial projections, inspect and resolve validation findings, and generate reconciled PDF/Word/Excel outputs whose material figures agree with the same calculation run.
